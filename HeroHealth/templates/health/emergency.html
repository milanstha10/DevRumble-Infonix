Emergency First-Aid Triage Guide

RED Critical Alert

Chest pain, unconsciousness, massive bleeding, severe difficulty breathing, sudden stroke-like symptoms.

➔ CALL 102 / 100 IMMEDIATELY

YELLOW High Priority

Severe abdominal pain, fracture without visible bone, high fever (>103°F) unresponsive to medicine, persistent vomiting.

➔ Head to nearest emergency clinic

GREEN Low Priority

Minor cuts, mild cold/cough, muscle aches, mild skin allergies.

➔ Schedule GP visit or home rest
